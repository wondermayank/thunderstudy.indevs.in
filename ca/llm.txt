# ThunderStudy — CA Exam Prep (/ca)
> Free, ad-light study material for India's Chartered Accountancy (CA) exams, published by ThunderStudy (thunderstudy.indevs.in).

## What ThunderStudy is
ThunderStudy is a free Indian exam-prep platform. This file describes the CA
(Chartered Accountancy) section at https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/ca — one
vertical of a larger site that also covers other Indian competitive/academic
exams. Everything under /ca is free: no login, no paywall, no signup wall.

## Exams covered under /ca
- CA Foundation — all 4 subjects (Accounting, Business Law, Quantitative
  Aptitude, Business Economics): chapter-wise notes, PYQs, and a study planner.
- CA Inter — both groups: chapter-wise notes and PYQs.
- CA Final — chapter-wise notes and PYQs.

## Site structure (key pages)
- /ca — hub page for all three CA levels, formula sheets, and notes.
- /ca/foundation — CA Foundation overview, syllabus, and subject cards.
- /ca/foundation/notes — CA Foundation chapter-wise notes by subject (interactive).
- /ca/inter — CA Inter overview, syllabus, and subject cards.
- /ca/final — CA Final overview, syllabus, and subject cards.
- /ca/notes — cross-level notes hub (Foundation / Inter / Final).
- /ca/special — CA Foundation study planner tool (generates a day-by-day
  revision schedule from a student's exam date and off days).
