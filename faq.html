Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ThunderStudy's free study material, books, mock tests, privacy and support.

What is ThunderStudy?
ThunderStudy is a free Indian study platform offering notes, books, previous-year questions, sample papers and mock tests for school boards and competitive examinations.
Is ThunderStudy free?
Yes. ThunderStudy resources are free to use and do not require a paid subscription or login.
Do I need to create an account?
No. ThunderStudy does not require registration or login for its study material, books and mock-test resources.
Does ThunderStudy show advertisements?
ThunderStudy is designed to provide its study resources without display advertisements.
Does ThunderStudy collect personal data?
ThunderStudy does not intentionally collect personal data. Progress, scores and preferences used by supported tools are stored locally in the browser.
Which exams and subjects are covered?
ThunderStudy covers CBSE, NCERT, CUET, JEE, NEET, CAT and MBA entrance, NDA, CLAT and AILET, CA, Diploma courses, TET and CTET, UPSC, SSC, Banking and related examinations.
Does ThunderStudy provide CBSE and NCERT material?
Yes. The platform includes CBSE Class 10 and 12 resources, NCERT books and solutions for Classes 6 to 12, including Hindi-medium material where available.
Where can I find CUET, JEE and NEET preparation?
Use the relevant exam sections from the ThunderStudy home page. They include notes, formula sheets, subject material, previous-year questions and practice resources where available.
Where can I take mock tests?
Mock tests are available through the ThunderStudy CBT platform.
Are the downloadable books free?
Yes. ThunderStudy links to free downloadable books and study guides, including board, CUET and general-knowledge resources where available.
Can I use ThunderStudy on a mobile phone?
Yes. ThunderStudy pages are responsive and can be used from modern mobile browsers, tablets, laptops and desktop computers.
Can I use ThunderStudy offline?
Some files can be downloaded for offline study. Interactive pages and mock tests generally require an internet connection unless your browser has already cached the required resources.
How do I report a broken link or incorrect information?
Report broken links, outdated exam details or content errors through the contact page so the page can be reviewed and corrected.
Who created ThunderStudy?
ThunderStudy was created and is maintained by Wondermayank, an Indian developer, designer and educator. The platform began in 2023.
How often is ThunderStudy updated?
Pages are updated when new study material, exam information, links or corrections become available. Official references are preferred when they are available and cited.
Can I request a study file or topic?
Yes. Use the Request File or Contact link to send a request. Availability depends on the topic, source material and review requirements.
Is ThunderStudy affiliated with an exam board or government department?
ThunderStudy is an independent educational resource platform. It is not an official exam board or government department unless a page explicitly identifies an official source.
How can I support ThunderStudy?
You can share useful resources with students, report errors, request needed material and follow the official ThunderStudy channels.

Still have a question?

For corrections, file requests or other support, contact Wondermayank.
