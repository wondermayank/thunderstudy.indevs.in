## What is ThunderStudy
ThunderStudy (https://thunderstudy.indevs.in) is a free Indian competitive-exam
preparation platform built by a solo student developer (Wondermayank,
https://wondermayank.in). It provides notes, previous year questions (PYQs),
syllabus breakdowns, exam-pattern guides, and computer-based mock tests
(via https://thundertest.indevs.in) for CBSE, CUET, JEE/NEET, SSC, Banking,
CAT, CLAT, NDA and CA Foundation aspirants. Everything is free, ad-light,
and does not require login for study material.
