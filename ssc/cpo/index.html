Frequently asked questions

Is this material really free?

Yes — all ThunderStudy resources are completely free. No login, no paywall.

What posts are recruited under SSC CPO?

SSC CPO recruits for Sub-Inspector (SI) in Delhi Police and Sub-Inspector in CAPFs (BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB, NIA, SSF).

Are the PYQs solved with explanations?

Yes, each question comes with a step-by-step solution so you understand the concept, not just the answer.

What is Paper-II of SSC CPO?

Paper-II is an English Language & Comprehension paper (200 marks, 2 hours) taken after clearing Paper-I and the Physical Endurance Test.
