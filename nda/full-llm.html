Free NDA exam preparation resources

ThunderStudy provides free NDA Mathematics and General Ability Test study resources for Indian aspirants. The site is maintained by Thunder / wondermayank and is not an official UPSC website.

What is available?

Students can use concise notes, formula sheets, previous-year question papers and sample papers, then practise with the linked computer-based mock-test platform.

  • NDA preparation hub: overview and links to all resources.
  • NDA notes: subject and topic study notes.
  • NDA formula sheets: Mathematics and GAT revision formulas.
  • NDA PYQ papers: previous-year Mathematics and GAT question papers.
  • NDA sample papers: full practice sets in PDF format.

Who is it for?

These pages are for students preparing for the UPSC NDA written examination and for anyone looking for free revision material, solved-paper practice and mock-test links.
