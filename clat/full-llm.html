CLAT & AILET Law Entrance Preparation

URL: https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/clat/ — Free CLAT and AILET preparation: legal reasoning notes, constitution principles, current affairs, logical aptitude, and previous year question papers.

CLAT Notes (/clat/notes.html)

Chapter-wise notes across five subject areas:

  • English — 19 chapters (Nouns, Pronouns, Tenses, Subject Verb Agreement, Articles, Adjectives, Question Tag, Preposition, Adverb, Commonly Mis-spelt Words, Idioms, One Word Substitution, Antonyms & Synonyms, Phrasal Verbs, Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Parajumbles, Active Passive Voice, Narration)
  • General Knowledge — 7 sub-subjects, 70+ chapters: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Geography, History, Polity
  • Legal Reasoning — 5 sub-sections: Law of Crimes, Law of Tort, Constitution of India, Indian Contract Act, Miscellaneous Laws
  • Logical Reasoning — 18 chapters (Analogy, Odd One Out, Missing Term in Series, Blood Relation, Syllogism, Coding Decoding, and more)
  • Quantitative Techniques — 22 chapters (Number System, LCM/HCF, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Time & Work, Data Interpretation, Mensuration, Trigonometry, and more)

CLAT Previous Year Questions (/clat/pyqs.html)

Year-wise CLAT papers from 2016 to 2026 (11 years), four question sets per year (Set A, B, C, D) — 44 papers total.

AILET Previous Year Questions (/clat/ailet-pyqs.html)

AILET papers from 2016 to 2026 (11 years), 150 questions per paper, each with a detailed answer key and topic-wise analysis.

CLAT Sample Papers (/clat/sample-paper.html)

Six full-length sample papers (Paper I–VI), 120 questions each across English Language (40), Current Affairs & GK (35), Logical Reasoning (40), and Legal Reasoning (5), all with solutions.
