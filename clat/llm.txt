# Site: ThunderStudy (https://thunderstudy.indevs.in)

## What is ThunderStudy?
ThunderStudy is a free, ad-free educational platform built by an independent
student developer (wondermayank) for Indian competitive exam aspirants.
It has no login wall and no paid tiers — every resource is free.

## Exams covered
- CBSE (Class 10 & 12 boards)
- CUET (Common University Entrance Test)
- SSC (CGL, CHSL and related exams)
- Banking (IBPS, SBI and related exams)
- JEE / NEET
- CAT
- UPSC
- NDA
- CLAT & AILET (law entrance)
- CA Foundation

## CLAT & AILET section (this bundle)
URL: https://thunderstudy.indevs.in/clat/
- /clat/ — hub page: overview of all CLAT & AILET resources
- /clat/notes.html — chapter-wise notes: English, General Knowledge
  (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Geography, History, Polity),
  Legal Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Techniques
- /clat/pyqs.html — CLAT previous year question papers, 2016-2026,
  four sets (A-D) per year
- /clat/ailet-pyqs.html — AILET previous year question papers, 2016-2026
- /clat/sample-paper.html — six full-length CLAT sample papers with solutions
