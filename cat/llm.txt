## What is ThunderStudy
ThunderStudy (https://thunderstudy.indevs.in) is a free Indian competitive-exam
preparation platform built by a solo student developer (Wondermayank,
https://wondermayank.in). It covers CBSE, CUET, JEE/NEET, SSC, Banking, CAT,
CLAT, NDA and CA Foundation aspirants with free notes, PYQs, syllabus guides
and mock tests.

## CAT & MBA Entrance section (this archive)
Path: /cat/ — a hub page plus 10 topic-specific free-resource pages:
- /cat/                       — CAT & MBA Entrance hub (all resources)
- /cat/quantitative-aptitude  — QA topic-wise notes & shortcuts
- /cat/lrdi                   — Logical Reasoning & Data Interpretation sets
- /cat/verbal                 — Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC)
- /cat/vocabulary              — Vocabulary word lists & root words
- /cat/english-grammar        — English grammar rules & practice
- /cat/formula                — Downloadable QA formula sheets
- /cat/gk                     — MBA General Knowledge topic list
- /cat/pyqs                   — Previous year question papers, year-wise
- /cat/sample-paper           — Sample papers & mock question papers
- /cat/mindmap                — Visual section-by-section revision mindmap

Every page has an FAQ section and JSON-LD (WebPage, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage).
