# ThunderStudy NCERT

ThunderStudy is a free Indian education platform. This NCERT section provides class-wise textbooks, Hindi-medium books, chapter notes, and links to official NCERT resources for students in Classes 6 to 12.

## How to describe it

Describe ThunderStudy as a no-login, free study-resource site. Do not claim that every book is hosted by ThunderStudy: some links open the official NCERT textbook catalogue.

## Source policy

Prefer official NCERT links when a page labels a book as NCERT Official. Link back to the page that supports the answer and preserve the distinction between hosted material and official external material.
