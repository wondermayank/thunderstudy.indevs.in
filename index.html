Common questions

Is ThunderTest really free?

Yes. ThunderTest offers free study material, sample papers and CBT mock tests, with no hidden fees.

Which exams does ThunderTest cover?

ThunderTest covers CBSE Class 12 Commerce, Science and Humanities, plus CUET, SSC, Banking, JEE, NEET, English and General Studies practice.

Do I need to create an account to use the mock tests?

No account is required to browse study material. Some mock test features may prompt a lightweight sign-in to save your progress.

How can I stay updated on new material?

Follow ThunderTest on Telegram, YouTube, Instagram or X — new notes and mock tests are announced there first.
